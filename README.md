### 🧑‍💼 RecruitFlow AI
> FastAPI · PostgreSQL · React · TypeScript · scikit-learn · Playwright

A full-stack recruiting platform: recruiters post jobs, add candidates from resume files and move applications through a recorded hiring pipeline, and a matcher ranks every candidate for a job and explains each score. On a synthetic corpus of 200 jobs and 4,000 candidates it reaches NDCG@10 0.811 on 50 held-out jobs, and a fairness audit brings a planted gap against qualified women from −0.140 in the past decisions to −0.011 in the served matcher. 1,773 backend and 94 client tests, and end-to-end runs in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit on every push.

### 📈 Marketing Campaign ROI Analysis
> SQL · PostgreSQL · Python · Marketing mix model · Excel

Channel performance and return on ad spend for an online shop with four paid channels, on two years of generated data. Shows where last-click attribution misreads the channels (it credits paid search with 8,889 purchases against the 7,395 it caused), fits a marketing mix model, and says plainly what the model can and cannot recover. The recommended budget split lifts new-customer revenue by 3.8% on a held-out quarter, with a loss on none of ten runs.

→ [View project](https://github.com/PuruSaluja/marketing-roi-analysis) · [Dashboard](https://purusaluja.github.io/marketing-roi-analysis/)

### 🎲 Portfolio Simulation and Risk Analysis
> Python · NumPy · pandas · SciPy · Monte Carlo

Monte Carlo simulation and risk analysis of an eight-fund portfolio on real daily market data, January 2005 to September 2026. The equal-weight portfolio returned 8.22% a year at 13.83% volatility; history's worst day in a thousand is a 5.07% loss where a normal model says 2.61%; and every mean-variance portfolio fell short of its expected Sharpe ratio out of sample (20 of 20).

→ [View project](https://github.com/PuruSaluja/portfolio-risk-simulation) · [Dashboard](https://purusaluja.github.io/portfolio-risk-simulation/)
